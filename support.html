Where is my data stored?

Poopal is designed to keep your personal records under your control. Depending on the features and settings you use, records may be stored locally on your Apple devices and may use supported Apple services for synchronization.

Can I export my data?

If data export is available in your version of Poopal, you can use the export options inside the app to create supported report or data files.

Can Poopal send my data to an AI?

Poopal does not automatically send your bowel records to third-party AI services. If you export your data, you decide whether and where to share the exported file.

Does Poopal provide medical advice?

No. Poopal is intended for personal tracking and informational purposes. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

How do I report a problem?

Please email us and include your Poopal version, iOS or watchOS version, device model, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when appropriate.
