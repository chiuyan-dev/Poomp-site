Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Poomp is designed with privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Poomp.

Data You Record

Poomp allows you to record information about your bowel movements and related activity. This information is used to provide the app's tracking, history, and related features.

Data Storage

Poomp is designed to keep your personal records on your devices and, when applicable, in your personal iCloud account through Apple's services.

Poomp does not operate its own server for storing your personal bowel movement records.

iCloud

If iCloud synchronization is available and enabled, your Poomp data may be stored and synchronized through Apple's iCloud services associated with your Apple Account. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Analytics and Tracking

Poomp does not use your bowel movement records for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data Sharing

Poomp does not sell your personal bowel movement records to third parties.

Data Export

Poomp may allow you to export information or reports. Any information you choose to export is handled according to the destination or service you choose to share it with.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Poomp evolves. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
