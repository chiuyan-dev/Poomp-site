Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Poopal is designed to help you record and understand your bowel habits while respecting the privacy of your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Poopal.

1. Bowel Movement Records

Poopal may allow you to record information such as the date and time of a bowel movement, duration, stool consistency, and other information that you choose to enter.

Poopal is designed to keep your personal records under your control. The developer does not sell your bowel movement records to advertisers or data brokers.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Poopal may store your records locally on your Apple devices so that features such as history, statistics and trends can be provided.

Data stored locally is subject to the security and privacy protections provided by your device and operating system.

3. iCloud

If Poopal provides iCloud synchronization, eligible app data may be synchronized using Apple's iCloud services when that feature is enabled and available.

Information handled by iCloud is also subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices.

4. Apple Health

If you choose to connect Poopal with Apple Health, Poopal will request access only to Health data necessary for the features you choose to use.

Access to Apple Health data requires your explicit permission. You can review or revoke permissions through Apple Health or your device settings.

Health data obtained through HealthKit is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

5. Data Export

Poopal may allow you to export your records in formats such as PDF, CSV, or JSON.

Exported files are created at your request. You decide where those files are saved and with whom or with which services they are shared.

6. AI Services

Poopal does not automatically send your bowel movement records to third-party artificial intelligence services.

If you export your data and choose to provide it to an AI service or another third party, that information will be handled according to the privacy policy and terms of that third party.

7. Analytics and Tracking

Poopal does not use your bowel movement records for advertising or cross-app tracking.

If anonymous diagnostics or analytics are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the information involved and how it is used.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Records stored by Poopal remain available until they are deleted by you, removed through features provided by the app, or removed as a result of deleting associated app data.

Where synchronized services are used, deletion behavior may also depend on the settings and functionality of those services.

9. Children's Privacy

Poopal is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server.

10. Medical Disclaimer

Poopal is intended for personal tracking and informational purposes. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

If you have concerns about your health, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Poopal changes. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
